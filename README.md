Hi, I'm Harish. I'm a software engineer at [Purgo AI](https://www.purgo.ai/) in the San Francisco Bay Area, working on agents, retrieval and evals ([write-ups](https://harishmanoharan.com/work)).

I studied biology and then data science at IIT Madras. As a student I worked on estimating cell types in tumors from gene expression and on modeling how microbes in homes support each other, and I wrote my master's thesis with The Jackson Laboratory on finding behaviors in mouse videos without labels. After that came an M.S. in computer science at UT Dallas.

Things I've built on my own:

- **[ReadableSTEM](https://readablestem.com)** turns STEM course PDFs into accessible web pages, with equations as MathML a screen reader can speak. It's a free, open-source beta that anyone can sign in to. I evaluated it on a pre-registered held-out set of 15 OpenStax sections: heading F1 0.96 and 97% of figure descriptions passed by a model judge, while on whole-page equations a Claude Opus chatbot still scores higher, 0.62 to its 0.50 ([write-up](https://harishmanoharan.com/work/readablestem/), [source](https://github.com/harishm17/readablestem)).
- **[Margins](https://studymargins.com)** turns your own course files into notes, worked examples, quizzes and timed practice exams, each citing the pages it came from, plus a voice coach. On a retrieval eval of 38 topics from three OpenStax textbooks, its hybrid search finds 99% of the relevant passages in the top 24 ([source](https://github.com/harishm17/margins)).
- **[Holdbrief](https://holdbrief.com)** is a daily brief on the stocks you follow, for US and Indian markets, free during its beta. Fixed scoring methods set each stock's stance, and Claude writes the explanation; every sentence has to cite the facts behind it, and a checker rejects any that don't.
